🧭 Direction check — once per machine, no cutting
  1. X: from X0, G0 X50 must move the cutter away from the chuck centre.
  2. Rotary: mark the chuck face right under the cutter, then G0 C90 (C0 first). Looking at the chuck face from the cutter's side, the mark must turn clockwise (the part turns, so the cutter moves counter-clockwise over it — the ISO / LinuxCNC rule).
  3. Both right → leave both boxes unticked. One wrong → best fix it in your controller (e.g. the sign of STEP_SCALE in the LinuxCNC .ini), so jogging matches too; tick the box only if you cannot.
  4. A mirrored part passes every software check. Prove it once with an asymmetric test cut (e.g. the letter F) before a real part.
Axis velocities and accelerations are not set here: your controller enforces its own limits on every move.
